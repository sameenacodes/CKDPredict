About CKDPredict

Chronic Kidney Disease Prediction

An AI-powered clinical decision-support platform that uses machine learning to assess CKD risk from patient clinical parameters and provide interpretable assessment insights.

What is CKDPredict?

CKDPredict is a machine-learning based chronic kidney disease risk assessment system. It accepts selected patient clinical parameters, processes the inputs, and generates a CKD prediction with probability, confidence, explainable-AI factors and decision-support information. The platform also supports patient assessment history, progression monitoring, eGFR estimation, report generation and WhatsApp sharing.

Key Modules

Integrated features of the CKDPredict platform

🤖

ML Prediction

Uses a trained machine learning model to classify the patient's CKD risk from clinical input parameters.

🔎

Explainable AI

Shows important model features and helps explain why the model produced the assessment result.

📊

Model Validation

Provides model performance information including accuracy and other validation metrics.

📈

Risk Trend

Stores patient assessments and helps visualize changes in risk across previous assessments.

🧬

eGFR & GFR Stage

Estimates glomerular filtration rate using age, gender and serum creatinine and displays the corresponding GFR category.

🩺

Decision Support

Provides rule-based recommendations and warnings based on the assessment inputs and prediction.

📄

PDF Report

Generates a structured assessment report containing patient information, results and clinical insights.

💬

WhatsApp Sharing

Opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled assessment summary for convenient report sharing.

🔐

Assessment History

Maintains saved patient assessments so previous results can be reviewed and compared.
